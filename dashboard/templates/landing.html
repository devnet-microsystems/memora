🧠 Memora

Memora è un assistente AI progettato per supportare le persone con decadimento cognitivo lieve (MCI) e i loro caregiver. Through a natural voice interface, it helps patients remember their habits, building a persistent Memory Graph that caregivers can monitor to evaluate health status over time.

🎙️

Patient Mode

Simple voice interface. Talk to Memora to remember habits, orient yourself, or ask for help.

📊

Modalità Caregiver

Analytical Dashboard. Monitor the Memory Graph, manage memories, and generate weekly reports.
