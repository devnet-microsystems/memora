The Anomaly Flag (Recorded Use Case)

A key feature of Memora is deterministic anomaly detection for Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI) patients.

  • 1. The Trigger: In our automated seed script, the patient asks about their blood pressure medication 3 times within an hour.
  • 2. The Detection: The MemoryGraph calculates cosine similarity (via Nemotron Embeddings) over a rolling time window.
  • 3. The UI Result: If you visit the Caregiver Dashboard right now, you will see a bright Red Flag Node generated automatically to alert the caregiver.
